# Explicit within-slice moment counts

This expands the short moment explanation in PROOF.md. In a five-dimensional affine slice there are121 projective linear directions. A pair of distinct points has40 directions annihilating its nonzero difference and therefore81 directions placing the pair in different levels. Thus each unordered pair contributes81 to the sum of E2.

For three distinct cap points, choose one as origin for differences u,v. They are linearly independent; otherwise the three points would be a complete affine line over F3. A linear form places the three points in three different levels exactly when (b(u),b(v))=(1,2) or(2,1). Each assignment extends to3^(5-2)=27 linear forms, and division by the two nonzero scalar multiples leaves27 projective directions. Thus each unordered triple contributes27 to E3.

For a six-dimensional slice in the outer refinement the same argument gives3^5=243 for pairs in different levels and3^4=81 for triples in distinct levels. For mixed triples crossing the three anchor slices, the nonzero vector sum has40 annihilating projective directions in dimension5, or121 in dimension6. These establish every F6 and F7 coordinate in the certificate.
