# Accepted complete17-point4D histogram family

For an affine cap S of17 points in F3^4, the published17-cap section lemma guarantees a parallel section triple of sizes980,971,or881. We use that published lemma, the accepted maximum9 for caps in F3^3, and the independently checked complete affine orbit catalogue: one orbit of9-caps and three orbits of8-caps.

Choose layer coordinates t=0,1,2 and write S=(A,0) union(B,1) union(C,2). An affine change normalizes A to an orbit representative. If C is empty, B is any cap of the required size; there is no cross-layer restriction. If C={z}, after normalizing A by x→Lx+v, use x→Lx+v+t(Lz+v). The t=0 layer remains normalized and the singleton in t=2 becomes0, since3(Lz+v)=0. The transformed cap condition across all three layers is exactly B disjoint(-A). All other possible collinear triples lie within one layer. Thus the catalogues of B used in the finite replay cover every possible S after an affine transformation. The transformation preserves its full directional histogram.
