# Independent verification of3D cap histograms, cardinalities0..7

Purpose: verify P's complete new low-dimensional family input for L's4D16 model. Root recursively constructs every cap by adding its points in strictly increasing order, updating the forbidden third points from all pairs. This generator differs from P's complete subset scan and does not read its cap lists. Every partial cap of size0..7 is emitted once; all13 direction partitions and their sorted sizes are computed by independently built coordinate masks. Representatives are retained for every histogram. Root will compare the exact histogram sets/counts with P's independently frozen tables. Existing8/9classifications are not rerun. Budget120seconds,1GiB; only a verification/input family statement, no new cap bound or priority claim.
